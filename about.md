Hi, I'm Nathan, a second-year PhD student in the Theory of Computation group at MIT, advised by the inimitable [Ryan Williams](https://people.csail.mit.edu/rrw/). I did my undergrad at MIT as well. A list of papers I've coauthored can be found [here](/papers). I'm currently interested in AI safety, both in theory and in practice. I have also worked on space-bounded complexity theory and graph algorithms.
